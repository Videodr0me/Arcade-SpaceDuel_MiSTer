# Changelog

## Initial Release - 2026-11-24

- **Atari's Space Duel (1982)** on MiSTer, with the original 1.512 MHz 6502, Atari Analog Vector Generator and dual POKEY sound. Rev 2 is the main arcade entry. Rev 1 and the Prototype are available as alternatives.
- **Solo and simultaneous two-player play** with the cabinet's fighter and linked space-station modes. Each player has independent rotate, thrust, fire and shield controls.
- **Vector video at 1080p and 720p**, with optional 120 Hz output at 720p. CRT and Direct Video setups support 240p, 480i and 480p, with horizontal and vertical picture positioning.
- **Selectable authentic 61.52 Hz output** alongside the default 60 Hz presentation. Rotation, mirroring and wide framing accommodate different cabinet and monitor setups.
- **Authentic Vector Engine** with CRT glow, phosphor trails, drawing-speed compensation, brightness accumulation, white spill, color space conversion and a simulated slot mask. Independent controls adjust bloom, halo, within-frame decay and between-frame persistence.
- **Five video presets and two custom slots.** The presets adapt to the output resolution. Custom 1 starts with 80s Cruise Control and Custom 2 with 80s Overdrive. Keyboard shortcuts let you cycle presets or copy a favorite into either slot.
- **Selectable PCB audio filtering** reproduces the sound shaping on the original game board. The filter is on by default.
- **Original operator controls and high-score saving.** Service mode, diagnostic step and DIP switches are available. The defaults match Atari's recommended factory settings of four ships, Normal difficulty, a bonus ship every 10,000 points, and one coin per credit. Autosave is enabled by default and saves high scores while the OSD menu is open. Manual saving is also available.

Requires a DE10-Nano MiSTer with a 32 MB or larger SDRAM module.
